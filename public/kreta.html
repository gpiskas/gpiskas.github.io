Optional itinerary

  • Day 1 (15.9)
    Arrival, sleep, Heraklion + Matala beach
    Arrive early morning, rest at hotel, explore Heraklion and the famous Matala beach with its scenic cliffs.
  • Day 2 (16.9)
    Rethymno + Spilies beach, arrival Chania
    Drive to Rethymno, visit Spilies beach, then continue to Chania for the night.
  • Day 3 (17.9)
    Balos beach
    Full day boat trip to stunning Balos beach (possibly off-road too).
  • Day 4 (18.9)
    Elafonissi + Kedrodasos beaches
    Visit the popular pink-sand Elafonissi beach and the pristine Kedrodasos beach nearby.
  • Day 5 (19.9)
    Falassarna beach
    Explore Falassarna beach (can get windy). Scenic western coast experience.
  • Day 6 (20.9)
    Seitan Limani beach or nearby exploration
    Relax at Seitan Limani or explore other nearby coastal treasures before departure.
  • Day 7 (21.9)
    Departure
    Enjoy the day somewhere nearby until it's time to return the car and fly back.
